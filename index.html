The problem

Switzerland has some of the best golf in Europe — and almost no way to find a fourth. Clubs are private, apps are foreign, and most rounds still get booked in a WhatsApp group you had to already know about.

Golf Switzerland started as a content project — course reviews, swing content, the odd rant about slow play — and grew into something bigger: a place to actually meet the golfers behind the feed. The app is next. This is where it starts.
