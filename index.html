About Golf Switzerland

The problem

Switzerland has some of the most beautiful golf in Europe — but finding someone to play with is harder than it should be. Whether you're brand new or chasing a lower handicap, it's the right people who really make the round.

Golf Switzerland started as a content project — course reviews, golf swings, and beginner struggles. Then it grew into an actual community: a place to find fun people you can enjoy golfing with.
